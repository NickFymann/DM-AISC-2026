An infrastructure pilot completed all 24 method/scenario jobs using independent fitting, validation and test records. It covers only $M\in\{2,4\}$, one repetition, the cubic/cubic and tanh/cubic--quintic cases, and clean or 35 dB PIM-to-noise observations. Table~\ref{tab:pilot} reports its untouched-test scores; these pilot results do not provide means, variability or the final benchmark comparison.
