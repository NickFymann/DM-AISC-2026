# Related-work verification — 1 October 2026

The revised Introduction distinguishes the following primary sources. Claims are restricted to the scope supported by their article abstracts or accepted manuscripts; cancellation numbers from different setups are not treated as direct method benchmarks.

- Crespo-Cadenas et al. (2025), *A Sparse Bayesian Technique to Learn the Frequency-Domain Active Regressors in OFDM Wireless Systems*, Sensors 25(14), 4266, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/s25144266). The authors describe frequency-domain SBL regressor selection and distortion prediction for OFDM power-amplifier modeling. This is related nonlinear RF identification, not the same receiver-filtered PIM target. Evidence: [author-hosted article](https://idus.us.es/server/api/core/bitstreams/4c86bf7f-0070-439b-8060-722e361506e1/content), indexed full-text abstract; [publisher](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/25/14/4266). The live publisher fetch was rate-limited; bibliographic and abstract content was available from the author repository/search index.
- Liu et al. (2024), *Digital cancellation of multi-band passive inter-modulation based on Wiener–Hammerstein model*, Digital Communications and Networks 10(4), 1189–1197, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcan.2024.06.002). The [publisher abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2352864824000695) describes a multi-band structural model using FIR filters, basis generation and B-splines. This differs from screening an otherwise fixed Volterra dictionary. The indexed publisher abstract was available; direct page access returned 403.
- Wei (2025), *System identification and interpretable modelling of dynamical systems with small data using sparse Bayesian learning*, IWSSIP, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/IWSSIP66997.2025.11151937). The [accepted manuscript](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/229977/1/IWSSIP-2025%20ID34%20Final%20Accepted%20Manuscript.pdf), Sections III.C and IV, describes NARX identification and SBL refinement with BIC/PESR. Its real-data application differs from this paper's synthetic complex PIM regression. [Institutional metadata](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/229977/) confirms author, venue and DOI.

Existing references to measured air-induced PIM and cascade models are retained. The revised manuscript makes no claim that placing correlation screening before established SBL is a new inference principle.
